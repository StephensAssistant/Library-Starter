# Live Data-Visual Explanation

Explain an existing, evidence-validated chart or dashboard clearly during a live presentation.

## Intent-routing contract

This Skill owns live spoken explanation of data visuals. It does not own story invention, rehearsal, audience-resistance preparation or medium adaptation.

## When to use

Use while preparing the spoken explanation and navigation of an existing visual. Route story development to [[SKILL-0037]].

## Method

1. State the supported takeaway or decision before explaining detail.
2. Present to the audience, using deliberate silence while checking the visual or notes.
3. Orient the audience to metric, period, comparison and relevant encoding.
4. Explain in the sequence context → observation → meaning → action or question.
5. Use selective pace, silence or repetition for emphasis; do not read every label.
6. Separate slide navigation from message delivery.
7. Observe whether the audience can identify the takeaway, period/comparison, meaning and requested action without prompting; capture confusion, questions, attention loss or incorrect restatement as feedback evidence.

## Benchmark case

A sales-decline chart is introduced with its headline, period and comparison, followed by the observed pattern, supported meaning and required decision without a line-by-line chart tour.

Missing-input design case: the visual shows a decline but no validated causal explanation or approved action. Explain the observed comparison and its limits, then state the open question; confident delivery must not turn a speculative cause into a supported takeaway.

## Anti-patterns

- Reading labels or bullets verbatim
- Speaking while turned toward the screen
- Filling every pause
- Using confident delivery to hide analytical uncertainty
- Treating presenter confidence as proof that the audience understood the visual

## Sources

- https://www.storytellingwithdata.com/podcast/110

## Stop

Stop when the audience can follow the visual, supported meaning and requested action without unnecessary narration.
