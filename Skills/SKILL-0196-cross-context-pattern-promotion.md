# Cross-Context Pattern Promotion

Promotes a repeated evidence-backed behaviour to one canonical owner.

## When to use

Use when the same behaviour appears in at least two independent canonical assets or materially different contexts.

## Method

1. Record two distinct occurrence IDs with evidence.
2. Confirm independence and the shared behaviour.
3. Analyse the clean common owner.
4. Propose one shared rule rather than duplicated reminders. Complete a verified bounded Skill improvement under AGENTS.md’s standing capture authority when the existing Skill owns it; other policy or layer changes remain proposals pending their applicable authority.
5. Do not create relationships automatically.
6. If a prior fix failed, reopen it and diagnose the ineffective control.

## Decision and evidence details

Retain each occurrence's canonical ID, source/date, observed outcome and the shared behaviour being promoted. Explain why contexts are independent and why one owner covers the behaviour without merging different causes. A copied account of one event is not a second occurrence.

Boundary design case: two records repeat the same incident description. Treat them as one provenance chain until independent occurrences are evidenced; do not promote a cross-context rule from duplicated narrative.

## Benchmark case

The same missed verification occurs in a Playbook and Skill; propose one common owner after confirming both occurrences.

## Anti-patterns

- Counting repetition without outcome evidence
- Duplicating the rule everywhere
- Auto-linking the occurrences

## Stop

Stop after verified authorised Skill capture, or with a common-owner proposal, precise blocked dependency or evidence that the occurrences are not one pattern. Staging capture is not independent acceptance or merge.
