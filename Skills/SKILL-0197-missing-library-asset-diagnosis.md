# Missing Library Asset Diagnosis

Determines whether a reusable capability requires a new Hat, Playbook or Skill.

## When to use

Use when routing finds no clean current owner for required work.

## Method

1. Search current indexes, triggers, purposes and confirmed links; record a zero-match result.
2. Confirm the capability is reusable, materially distinct, evidence-backed, concise and testable.
3. Choose the layer: Hat for durable role/accountability, Playbook for repeatable sequence, Skill for one reusable technique.
4. Define one distinct boundary, source-backed method, known failure constraints, representative verification and stop condition. A verified reusable discovery may qualify after one worked occurrence; repetition is not required to capture a distinct proven technique.
5. For a missing Skill, automatically reserve its permanent ID through SKILL-0024, follow Skills/README.md, _TEMPLATE.md and _HOW-TO-CREATE.md, and stage the canonical Skill with intent-routing contract and benchmark. the Library owner's 4 October 2026 standing instruction authorizes this bounded creation; do not ask for a duplicate capture request. Prefer updating an existing owner when the capability already fits.
6. Record truthful verification and the final owner in the work checkpoint. If evidence, access, allocation or source-write capability is missing, preserve the exact pending gap without inventing a Skill/ID or declaring capture.
7. Do not expand execution, deployment, commit-playbook or merge authority, or force the capability into an ill-fitting asset. Missing Hats/Playbooks retain their own governed proposal route.

## Benchmark case

A single transformation technique is proven, reusable and has no suitable owner after search: reserve and stage its focused Skill automatically, rather than merely proposing it or creating a broad Playbook.

## Anti-patterns

- Declaring absence without search
- Creating a one-off asset
- Forcing distinct work into an existing owner

## Stop

Stop after the verified Skill is staged or its existing owner is updated, or with a precise evidence/access/allocation blocker. Missing Hats/Playbooks stop with their governed proposal.
