# SKILL-0073 — Trading Scale-Up Readiness Assessment

## Purpose

Decide whether evidence supports increasing strategy size or deployed capital.

## When to use

Use for: can I scale up trading; assess trading size increase; review capital deployment readiness.

## Method

1. Verify current configuration, protections and execution integrity.
2. Assess sample coverage across regimes, drawdown/recovery, stressed concentration and maximum margin use.
3. Confirm loss, liquidation and automatic de-risking consequences.
4. Apply the accepted readiness/risk criteria and verdict mapping to return GO, HOLD or NO-GO with evidence and the smallest unmet condition. If the mapping is absent, contradictory or unevaluable, return the precise criteria Gap without inventing a verdict or threshold.


### Application checks

Before a verdict, obtain the accepted readiness/risk criteria and their GO, HOLD and NO-GO mapping from the authorised policy owner. Map each condition to current configuration, execution, regime coverage and stress evidence. Distinguish failed conditions from missing evidence using that accepted mapping. If the mapping is absent, contradictory or unevaluable, return a criteria Gap instead of inventing a verdict or threshold. A readiness assessment does not authorise changing lot size, deployed capital or automatic de-risking controls.

## Benchmark case

Evaluate a profitable recent strategy sample and reject scale-up if it lacks regime coverage or stressed margin evidence despite strong headline returns.

## Anti-patterns

- Scaling to recover losses.
- Treating a short profitable window as sufficient.
- Ignoring rollback or de-risking behaviour.

## Stop

Stop with one evidenced readiness verdict and conditions for reassessment, or the precise criteria Gap that prevents a verdict.
