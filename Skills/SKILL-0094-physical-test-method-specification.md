# SKILL-0094 — Physical Test Method Specification

## Purpose

Specify the applicable physical test method, authority, equipment and evidence without fabricating execution.

## When to use

Use for: specify a physical test method; provide an NDT test procedure; define EMC or proof load evidence.

## Method

1. Identify the object, decision, jurisdiction and consequence.
2. Verify the current applicable standard or legal requirement.
3. State competent-person, equipment, calibration, environment and safety prerequisites.
4. Define method, sampling, acceptance criteria and evidence record.
5. Label the output HOW-TO until authorised personnel provide results.

## Benchmark case

Specify a proof-load test with current standard, qualified operator, calibrated equipment, acceptance criteria and evidence, while making no claim that it ran.

Missing-input design case: the applicable current standard or equipment calibration evidence cannot be obtained. Identify the dependent method or acceptance gap and leave the specification incomplete; do not invent a proof-load value, legal criterion or completed physical result.

## Anti-patterns

- Inventing physical results.
- Using an outdated standard.
- Giving unsafe execution instructions without authority controls.

## Stop

Stop with a verified method specification and explicit execution owner.
