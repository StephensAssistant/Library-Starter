# Article Shaping

Turn a thesis and source fragments into a coherent article using distinct beat-selection and structure-shaping modes.

## When to use and intent routing contract

Activate for shape an article argument, arrange writing beats or equivalent intent. Exclude minor copy edit only, publish an article automatically. Own this technique only; orchestration, approvals, release and communications remain with their current owners.

## Method

1. Establish audience, intended reader change, thesis, available evidence and desired format. Read the user’s style and canonical draft; keep unsupported claims visible.
2. In beat-selection mode, identify the minimum argument moments: reader problem, concrete example, claim, evidence, implication and useful ending. Test each beat for a job; cut repetition while retaining necessary qualifications.
3. In structure-shaping mode, compare plausible orders or forms against audience understanding. Use a clear opening promise, transitions that explain the connection and a conclusion earned by the evidence. Do not assume one universal template.
4. Place fragments against the chosen structure, mark evidence gaps, and rewrite within the user’s voice. Check that each claim is supported, each section advances the thesis and the ending delivers the opening promise. Preserve the source draft until the authorised update is verified.

## Anti-patterns

- A persuasive sequence that invents facts or removes uncertainty to sound stronger.
- Replacing the user’s style with a generic template; treating shaping as publishing authority.

## Benchmark cases

- Positive: Arrange an engineering lesson around a concrete failure, explanatory mechanism and practical consequence.
- Negative/boundary: A punctuation edit does not justify restructuring the argument or changing the thesis.

## Stop

Stop with a coherent structure or reviewable draft and named evidence gaps. Publication and distribution require their existing authority.

## Maturity

Upstream marks this method beta/in progress. This is a bounded Library adaptation, not evidence of production tooling reliability. Verify it against the actual task before relying on it.
