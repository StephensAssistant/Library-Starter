# Knowledge Handoff Continuity

## Trigger

A Ticket or Project advances, pauses, waits, changes owner or transfers between people/assistants.

## Method

Maintain an in-record checklist derived directly from the approved plan's stages, dependencies and acceptance points. After every completed stage—including minor but factual steps—tick the item and update the authoritative Ticket or Project with result/evidence, current state, decision or blocker, relevant artifact/link, owner or waiting-on party, and exact next action. On pauses or transfers, reconcile the checklist before stopping and name the successor only if evidenced. Keep affected customers and stakeholders informed through the appropriate communication playbook, including when waiting on a third party or system access; record communication only after confirmed. Provide the record link and preserve rejected paths when they prevent repeated work.

## Resume across assistants

Keep one compact working-state checkpoint in the existing owner, updating it in place: objective and excluded scope; accepted decisions and constraints; current provisional hypothesis with supporting/conflicting evidence and its next discriminating check; verified results and their source revisions; the single next domino and success condition; unresolved decisions or waiting dependencies. Include only fields that affect resumption. Preserve rejected approaches when their reason prevents repeated work. A hypothesis remains provisional until its check supports the stated conclusion.

On resume, open that owner and its used plan, check material sources for currency, and reconcile the latest user instruction before acting. Do not repeat settled questions or completed checks without changed evidence. After each domino, reassess downstream work and remove actions already made unnecessary. For competing work, use recorded priorities, deadlines and capacity to show the trade-off; missing capacity is a gap, not permission to invent a schedule. AGENTS.md owns priority and commitment authority.

Report whether the checkpoint was saved to staging or remains pending, and any acceptance still outstanding. Saving shared context does not establish that another assistant has read or applied it.

## Stop

The checklist and record truthfully show completed, current, waiting and next work, and affected people have the required update. No duplicate handoff document, session summary, nine-category inventory or role trail.

AGENTS.md owns shared authority, evidence reuse and task boundaries.

## Mandatory progress checklist

Every active Ticket and Project must keep one current checklist in its canonical record. When a plan exists, the checklist is its execution view: preserve the same stages, ordering, dependencies and acceptance points, and link the plan rather than inventing a parallel work breakdown. When the plan changes, update the plan and checklist together so they cannot drift. If no separate plan is warranted, the canonical record's scoped actions form the checklist. Each item records: stage/action; state (pending, active, complete, blocked or waiting); concise outcome; evidence or artifact; owner/waiting-on party; and next action. Update it in place whenever a stage completes or its state changes. Never leave completed work unticked, mark unverified work complete, or rely on chat as the only progress record.

A waiting update must state what is needed, from whom or which authorised system owner, why it blocks progress, the last confirmed action, and the next review or chase point when one is explicitly known. Do not invent dates, owners or commitments.
