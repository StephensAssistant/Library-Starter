# Stakeholder Communication — Translating Technical Work for a Non-Technical Audience

## Trigger

A stakeholder update is requested.

## Method

Before drafting, confirm the verified audience, channel, purpose, sensitivity, required decision/request, owner, deadline and approval constraints; ask only when a missing item materially changes the message. Use [[PLAY-0034]] and structure the substance as STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result. For unfinished work, Result states the honest current outcome, blocker or next action rather than implying completion. Write in natural human language; do not force STAR headings or formulaic phrasing unless the channel benefits from them. Lead with what the audience needs to understand or do, use necessary evidence in plain language, and include technical detail only when it changes the decision.

## Stop

Concise send-ready draft for the Library owner to review and send. Never send, schedule or publish; no separate framing, tone and filing chains.

AGENTS.md owns shared authority, evidence reuse and task boundaries.

## Matt Pocock method extension — 2026-10-10

If a stakeholder finds the draft confusing, change the explanation's entry point rather than repeating it verbatim. State the concrete effect in the recipient's terms, use one familiar example, then provide the minimum mechanism and requested decision. Preserve facts, uncertainty and the existing audience/communication contract. Re-pitching produces a revised draft; it does not grant sending authority or expand this Playbook into general technical work.
