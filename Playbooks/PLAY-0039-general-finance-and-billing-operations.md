# General Finance & Billing Operations

## Trigger

An invoice, expense, billing or ROI task is requested.

## Method

Verify the authoritative source, currency, period, tax/VAT treatment, accounting basis and every material figure. Show the calculation or reconciliation with its inputs, formula, source and date; identify discrepancies rather than forcing a balance. Provide an evidence-based result or recommendation only when AGENTS.md’s 95-point operational gate is met and no material gap remains. Below the AGENTS.md gate, use authorised sources to resolve discoverable gaps, then use SKILL-0318 for the current round of all independent decision-changing questions; dependent actions wait. The gate is the operational 30/30/20/20 rubric, not a probability or transaction authority. Prepare analysis and draft outputs only: the Library owner performs every payment, refund, purchase, transfer, submission or other financial transaction. Record a material decision or transaction only after the Library owner confirms it.

## Stop

Verified result satisfying the 95-point operational gate, or the precise missing figure, source, treatment or authority blocking the dependent conclusion. Never execute a financial transaction, invent a number, hide an unreconciled difference or file an unconfirmed decision.

AGENTS.md owns shared authority, evidence reuse and task boundaries.
