# Customer Communication — Follow-up Chase

## Trigger

An existing request is due for follow-up or the Library owner asks to chase.

## Method

Check original request, replies and agreed timing. Draft the outstanding ask under [[PLAY-0034]]. Return a send-ready draft for the Library owner to review and send. Do not invent a deadline.

## Stop

Send-ready follow-up drafted; record actual sending and the next review only after the Library owner confirms them. Silence does not close work.
