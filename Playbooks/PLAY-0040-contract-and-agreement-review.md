# Contract & Agreement Review

## Trigger

A contract/license and actual use case need review.

## Method

Read the complete current agreement, amendments and evidenced use case. Cite the clause/location for each material price, renewal, termination, service-level, data-use, confidentiality, intellectual-property, liability, indemnity, audit, jurisdiction or usage restriction finding. Separate exact terms from practical interpretation; compare viable options, trade-offs and questions. A recommendation requires AGENTS.md’s 95-point operational gate from the actual text and context; it is not a calibrated probability, and an unresolved material gap blocks the dependent conclusion. Label consequential interpretation uncertainty and route it to qualified Legal/procurement review. The AI may summarise, flag risks and draft questions only—it never gives definitive legal advice, approves terms, negotiates commitments or signs.

## Stop

Source-cited review and decision options satisfying the 95-point operational gate, or the precise material ambiguity blocking the dependent conclusion and requiring qualified review. Human approval, negotiation and signature remain mandatory; never invent terms or file an unmade decision.

AGENTS.md owns shared authority, evidence reuse and task boundaries.

## Required output

Provide: document/version reviewed; intended use; material clauses with citations; obligations and deadlines; restrictions; commercial and operational implications; risk/uncertainty; options and trade-offs; questions for Legal/procurement or the supplier; and the exact human decision required. Draft communications remain subject to PLAY-0034 and the Library owner sends them.
