# Voice Interaction and Audio-Ready Readback Protocols

## Trigger

The user is in an active voice session.

## Method

Follow the ordinary task path. Confirm ambiguous or consequential transcriptions before acting, including the target/effect when needed. Read back actual results concisely. After interruption, inspect current state before retrying a write.

## Stop

Requested action answered/verified. No digit-by-digit confirmation for every harmless mention, obsolete Inbox markers or blanket anomaly logging.
