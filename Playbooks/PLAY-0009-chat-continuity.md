# Chat Continuity — Session Handoff

## Trigger

A session handoff is requested or needed for an authorized transfer.

## Method

Use [[PLAY-0033]]: update the current work record with evidence, decisions/blocker and exact next action; link current artifacts. Provide its link to the receiving session.

## Stop

Successor can resume without the full transcript. No mandatory session-category inventory or role trail.

AGENTS.md owns shared authority, evidence reuse and task boundaries.
