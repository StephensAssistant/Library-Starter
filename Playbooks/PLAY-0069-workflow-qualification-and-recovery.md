# Workflow Qualification and Recovery

## Trigger and boundary

A workflow needs first qualification, fails read-only admission, has stale evidence/dependencies, or returns a recorded error requiring a component repair. This controller is outside the workflow it qualifies. Its internal output is an evidenced eligibility or refusal packet and handoff to Head Librarian, not execution of the target workflow. Eligibility/refusal describes the component attempt, not the overall service outcome; the original task remains open until completed success or awaiting_steve. Do not replay all unapproved FLOW phases as a disguised qualification run.

## Method

1. Head Librarian verifies the existing request/scope, exact workflow identity and revision, current actor/access and source state; appoints Director of Workflows for investigation. Read current Workflows guides, workflow/component contracts and owning work record/plan. Establish the outcome, permitted effects and applicable source-repair authority once, then check that they remain valid before each effect. Reuse that grant without asking the Library owner again for its covered steps. A stopped target invocation does not cancel a separate still-current source-repair request. Apply Two-outcome service contract to any reporting when authority/access is missing. Apply SKILL-0188; this Playbook supplies no extra authority.
2. Run the read-only admission check against exact canonical bytes. Record expected/observed result, source baseline, failed requirement and known effects. Admission failure means no operational workflow step may start. Already-executing workflow errors stop their dependent path under that workflow's existing declared failure branch. If an event has no recorded repair route, preserve evidence and ask Head Librarian to commission that missing route before using it.
3. Director investigates through SKILL-0313/0113/0115 and returns an internal repair request to Head Librarian. Classify the event using the ordered autonomy decisions below and then the event routing table; compare existing canonical owners before proposing changes. Head Librarian selects/assigns the required specialists, resolves sequencing and checks scope/authority. Director does not allocate other Hats, grant access or silently broaden the work.
4. Within the assigned scope use PLAY-0041 and SKILL-0193/0194/0197 for bounded knowledge/component corrections. Reuse, amend or create only a distinct verified missing component through its current guide/allocator. Preserve original purpose, authority, failure evidence and affected callers. Record exact stop/re-entry/idempotency and success checks. Never repair a source to make the check green by weakening its requirement or inventing evidence.
5. Use PLAY-0019 to qualify named primitives/operator procedures and representative normal/failure/boundary cases. Verify actual actor/access and required guard behaviour at the real boundary. Qualify specific operator decisions and effect boundaries before first target admission; do not demand an admitted whole-workflow run as its own approval prerequisite. A manual control needs an explicit executable-by-operator procedure and representative observed results, not an installed runner. Keep local/synthetic, actual fixture and actual platform evidence distinct. Manual operator controls may remain instruction with explicit per-run verification; do not label them automatically enforced. No privileged live rehearsal mode bypasses FLOW admission.
6. Director integrates the assigned results and Head Librarian coordinates an actual non-author review under PLAY-0024 and the Workflows gate. Review exact definition, complete executable/conditional dependencies and supporting Skills, policy/resource bytes, tests, authority, material ambiguity and guardrails. Guardrail Designer supports; review/acceptance remains separate from authors/integrators. An incomplete conditional branch blocks generic approval even when the present run would not use it.
7. Keep current/needs_review while a material gap remains. After actual required qualification and exact independent PASS, the authorised source owner may record approved with current evidence/pins. Re-run admission only after changed input/new verified evidence. Limit this controller to three changed-input correction cycles for the same authorised source scope; record each cause, change and result. The cap is a ceiling, not permission to retry or a transfer of PLAY-0056's separate repair budget. Exhaustion, missing authority, unavailable enforcement, contradictory requirements or unrelated/unproved source movement stops the affected invocation.
8. On admission success, return eligible definition/revision, evidence, remaining limits and the original request to Head Librarian. Head checks whether a current explicit workflow-entry request still covers the exact approved declared scope and its resolved baseline, including inherited component grants under AGENTS rule6; source construction/review alone is not invocation authority. Head checks whether that authority still permits a fresh workflow invocation; only then may the separate workflow begin. A prior terminal stop, changed scope, missing permission or expired authority cannot be revived by this Playbook. Existing authority must be used without asking the Library owner again when scope, effects and access remain covered. A fresh invocation requires its own valid basis; do not infer it from source-repair authority. Truly missing authority becomes a concrete bounded standing-policy proposal where allowed, rather than repeated per-step permission requests; hard-law exceptions retain their exact required form. Qualification success never automatically resumes a stopped step, starts candidate validation, Commit, Merge or schedules work.

## Intent and representative cases

Route qualify a library workflow, recover failed workflow admission or repair workflow qualification here. Exclude ordinary approved workflow execution, running Commit Ready, committing or merging source, and executing an unapproved workflow. Reading a failed admission and this controller is not invocation of the failed FLOW.

Normal: stale canonical hash→Director diagnoses→Head assigns repair/test/review→fresh evidence→admission retest→eligible handoff. Negative: missing live access or independent acceptance cannot be filled by a role switch or a high score. Boundary: unknown error or unrelated staging movement preserves effects and stops the dependent run; Head must verify any new invocation authority before restart. ## Stop

Return the internal eligibility/refusal packet to Head with exact definition/baseline, actual checks, preserved effects, unresolved owner/next action and remaining authority. Apply Two-outcome service contract to that packet. This Playbook does not invoke its target FLOW or grant sends, schedules, deployments, candidate validation, Commit or Merge.

## Required route investigation and specialist evidence

For Commit Ready, PLAY-0056 “Required route assessment before a blocker or permission question” is mandatory before any capability-exhaustion claim or permission request. Local checker execution MUST NOT be inferred to require a clone/worktree. Record the failed canonical requirement, observed capability/access evidence and disposition of each applicable supported alternative. A route not assessed remains unknown. A missing dispatch tool alone does not prove guarded native push unavailable. This requirement applies to the human-facing blocker claim as well as to the operational effect boundary; the assessor does not automatically enforce either claim.

Apply SKILL-0313 external execution-route qualification before declaring feasibility or exhausted recovery. Head must commission Director investigation of unavailable capability, denied permission or skipped/no-result triggers, then assess already-authorised supported routes. Record each route as available with evidence, unavailable with cause, or untried with its exact blocker; untried is not exhausted. A source-repair route may continue within its current grant while the target remains stopped. Do not ask the Library owner to supply discoverable knowledge or routine authority already covered.

Use concrete Guardrail, Reliability and applicable Security contributions, followed by independent acceptance. Record contributor identity, scope, actual evidence, findings/disposition and limits in the immutable qualification evidence. Different Hat names on one author's work do not create reviewer independence. Do not award operational-feasibility acceptance for a necessary unproved capability. Preserve historical source acceptance under its original limits; changed contracts/pins need new evidence and review.

For candidate validation use PLAY-0056's selected trigger and boundary rules. Do not manufacture source or an unqualified trigger-only write; only PLAY-0056's explicitly admitted validation-request transport may reuse a corrected tree under current operational authority. Never widen a source grant into Actions authority, rerun a deliberately skipped job on unchanged inputs, or resume a terminal invocation. Eligibility handoff names the exact supported future route and its remaining per-run gates. A real missing external capability still stops its dependent effect; investigate supported alternatives and return its precise route, evidence, owner and required change. If none is available within current bounds, root Head asks the Library owner the genuine access or next-step decision rather than ending with a bare blocker.

## Classify infrastructure reads before choosing recovery

Director identifies whether the event is transient side-effect-free transport before a gate result, a permanent access/protocol problem, deterministic validation failure or an uncertain write. Head uses only the existing tested handler for that class. PLAY-0056 owns finite prior-failure GET recovery; it does not waive unchanged-candidate, terminal, shared-budget or authority rules. Qualify transient-then-success, exhaustion/deadline, partial-page failure, wrong scope, permanent errors and unknown effects; absence of a verdict must never become a successful absence check. Preserve source evidence and capture the responsible existing Skill correction in a changed-input repair commit.

## Mandatory tracked repair and execution handback

Apply Workflows/README.md, Mandatory operating briefings, to these recovery operations using actual failure/effect evidence and the permitted return. Missing live access blocks live-dependent work, not supported source investigation, unambiguous authorised repair or available local checks. Preserve the live acceptance gap; never guess business policy, fabricate verification or bypass acceptance. Obtain the required non-author review and qualified handback before any eligible continuation.


This section owns the failure-to-repair-to-execution feedback loop. Apply it when a workflow returns an unhandled or unrecovered failure after its declared in-step handler is exhausted. A transient read that recovers inside its tested request budget continues normally; it does not require a separate defect Ticket. Apply Two-outcome service contract when the tracker is unavailable.

1. **Track before repair effects.** The executing AI preserves the failed workflow/run/attempt, exact source and heads, failed step/check, expected/observed result, diagnostics and known/uncertain effects. Search Tickets for the stable defect identity: workflow, failed canonical component/step and normalised signature or diagnosed cause. Reuse the existing owner; exact run/revision identifies an occurrence, not a new defect. When no existing owner fits, create one distinct governed defect Ticket using Tickets README/template/how-to and the serialized allocator; never guess an ID. Record outcome/repair definition of done, current authority, priority evidence or unresolved priority, remaining source/candidate budgets, responsible Director and executing AI/session. Deduplicate repeated observations. If allocation/write is unavailable, retain the precise pending-ticket blocker and evidence; do not claim a Ticket exists. Current authorised repair work may use its existing owner rather than wait for a duplicate record.
2. **Director investigates; Head assigns.** Director acknowledges the tracked defect, searches Library knowledge, distinguishes cause from uncertainty and gives Head the smallest supported repair request. Head assigns actual applicable domain experts, Guardrail and Reliability/Security where material, testing and independent acceptance. Record identities, findings and disposition in the Ticket. A role name or loaded contract is not completed work. Continue covered work without another routine permission question; investigate discoverable facts before asking the Library owner.
3. **Repair, prove and learn.** Amend the canonical owner or create a genuinely missing governed component, qualify the observed failure and affected normal/boundary cases, obtain required non-author acceptance and renew affected workflow evidence/pins/admission. Apply PLAY-0041/SKILL-0030 for one primary learning signal, diagnosed gap, exact changed owner or specific no-change disposition and prevention check. A staged fix alone is not operational success. Preserve scopes, effects, idempotency and each existing cumulative limit; Ticket reassignment, closure/reopening or a new run name cannot reset them.
4. **Resolve the repair at its real acceptance boundary.** Read the Ticket's current definition of done, used plan and Tickets/_HOW-TO-CLOSE.md. A dedicated source-defect Ticket may close only when its recorded diagnosis, correction, required regression/independent acceptance, canonical saved-source readback, lesson disposition and usable handback are proved. If live verification is part of that definition, retain awaiting verification until observed; never weaken acceptance to close. When an enclosing Ticket owns broader work, complete only the evidenced defect checkpoint and keep the parent open for its remaining outcome. Closing a repair does not certify target workflow completion or candidate-ready.
5. **Hand back and follow the result.** Head returns the verified packet to the executing AI, naming corrected approved revision, exact evidence, occurrence/repair Ticket, preserved/uncertain effects, idempotency/reconciliation checks, remaining scope and both cumulative budgets, current invocation basis, admission requirements and the exact next permitted action. Use the current execution context or an already-authorised internal execution handoff with verified recipient/channel; external communications retain AGENTS.md's draft-only boundary. Do not invent cross-session delivery. If the other AI is not reachable, persist the packet and mark handoff pending; claim receipt/use only when observed. The executing AI applies Active-phase continuation versus re-entry to the packet and current state, then records the actual permitted result back to the same owner. Candidate-specific correction follows PLAY-0056 Bounded repair continuation.
6. **Recur or escalate precisely.** If the same defect recurs, reopen its repair checkpoint/Ticket through current lifecycle rules, mark the attempted control ineffective, append the new occurrence and investigate before another changed attempt. A genuinely different diagnosed defect gets its own existing/new governed owner. Repeat the controller only within Method 7's correction-cycle budget and, where applicable, PLAY-0056 Bounded repair continuation's separate candidate accounting under AGENTS.md Library rule 6. Apply Active-phase continuation versus re-entry to a stopped attempt and Two-outcome service contract to the actual remaining outcome or human handoff.

The normal target is original-outcome completion with captured learning; a repair-only completion must return the original outstanding outcome rather than silently ending the work. This is a mandatory on-use operator procedure, not a newly installed background watcher, autonomous runner, message bus or schedule. Source construction does not invoke the target. Follow the existing method and ordered decisions above at every boundary; no additional business-phase node or parallel record system is introduced.


## Two-outcome service contract — 16 October 2026

This section owns the generic service-return and human-handoff contract under AGENTS.md Library workflow orchestration. Every FLOW exposes only completed success or awaiting_steve; failed, blocked, paused and cancelled attempts remain truthful internal diagnostics. Historical third-terminal evidence is retained as history, not current policy or live-success proof.

Bind the declared failure_handling fields to this Playbook's Mandatory tracked repair and execution handback procedure and Active-phase continuation versus re-entry gate. The schema/checker owns required keys, fixed handler identities and enums; callers bind those fields to precise owner sections and local evidence under Workflows/README.md Single-owner rule bindings. Apply the shared controller before an unrecovered failure's declared destination. Repair closure alone is not original-task completion. A distinct repeated unsupported repair process requires its own evidenced search/design/allocation; the shared hook alone is no reason to create another FLOW.

A noncompleted child returns its evidence, effects, limits and proposed question internally to parent recovery; only a completed child may satisfy its declared completed_only success return. Only root Head presents the human handoff after accessible facts, assigned expertise, supported alternatives and permitted repairs have been assessed. The packet carries question, why_needed, investigated_options, recommendation, owner the Library owner, preserved_effects, remaining_limits, next_entry and cancellation. Follow current SKILL-0318’s Method for the necessary genuine the Library owner-owned missing-information/access/policy/next-step question; explain the affected blocked dependency. Do not ask for discoverable facts, routine authority already granted or an invented question. Preserve the exact next-entry conditions and actual limits; no answer is presumed to waive hard law.

Cancellation is the immediate global override: stop effects and acknowledge the instruction through awaiting_steve without investigation/repair effects, a briefing/logging prerequisite or a fabricated question. Missing admission, ledger, tracker or write access never prevents truthful read-only reporting. Completed success requires the original outcome's actual evidence; a bare blocked/waiting report is not completion.

## Active-phase continuation versus re-entry — 17 October 2026

The executing AI may resume only the exact recorded incomplete operation in a still-active originating attempt after qualified handback, with current unchanged authority, scope, admission and frozen definition/dependency baseline, reconciled effects and remaining applicable limits. Do not replay accepted operations, adopt unrelated source, infer operational authority or reset a budget. A returned failed attempt, terminated invocation, frozen-instruction change, exhausted limit or hard stop denies continuation; preserve that stop and assess only a separately valid future entry through Method 8. Repair acceptance is not original-task completion.
