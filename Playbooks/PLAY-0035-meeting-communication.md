# Meeting Communication — Agenda, Capture, Recap

## Trigger

A meeting or its outcome is requested.

## Method

Identify the governing Ticket or Project, required decision/outcome and necessary participants. Read its current plan, checklist, evidence and unresolved decisions; use a minimal agenda linked to existing pre-read. Prepare any agenda, invitation or message for the Library owner to review, schedule and send. After the meeting, use the available transcript, written notes or the Library owner's direct summary to update the same canonical record with verified decisions, rationale, rejected options when they prevent repeated work, owners, actions, dependencies, evidenced dates and relevant supporting material; align its plan and checklist under [[PLAY-0033]].

## Stop

Send-ready meeting draft complete, or the canonical Ticket/Project and aligned checklist reflect the verified meeting outcome. Never schedule or send; never create a parallel minutes chain, fabricate a commitment or treat an idea, hypothesis or unconfirmed discussion as an approved decision.

AGENTS.md owns shared authority, evidence reuse and task boundaries.

## Meeting record minimum

- Purpose and decision/outcome sought.
- Participants required and each participant's role in the decision.
- Agenda and existing pre-read links.
- Verified decisions and concise rationale.
- the Library owner's direct meeting summary or update is valid first-party evidence; a transcript is not required. Record its date and context, preserve his level of certainty, and ask only when ambiguity could materially change the work.
- Proposed ideas and hypotheses, with source/context and status clearly marked as proposed—not approved. Preserve them when they could inform later design, architecture, investigation or development; promote them to decisions only after evidenced agreement.
- Rejected or deferred options only when they affect later work.
- Actions, owner, dependency, acceptance point and evidenced date.
- Changes to scope, priority, risk, plan or checklist.
- Unresolved questions and exact next action.

Use a separate document only when the Library owner explicitly requests a standalone meeting artifact; link it back to the governing Ticket or Project and keep the canonical decisions/actions there.
