# Customer Communication — Intake

## Trigger

An acknowledgement is requested or needed.

## Method

Read current customer state and external reference. Use [[PLAY-0034]] to draft an acknowledgement, combining a necessary information question when appropriate. Do not promise unconfirmed delivery.

## Stop

Send-ready draft delivered; the Library owner reviews and sends it. Record changed state only after the Library owner confirms it was sent.
