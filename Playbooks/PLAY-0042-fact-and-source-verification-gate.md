# Fact & Source Verification Gate

## Trigger

A material claim needs source verification.

## Method

Identify the consequential facts and inspect their real sources. Reuse valid evidence for unchanged inputs. Independently recheck when risk or actual acceptance requires it; correct affected claims and label unknowns.

## Stop

Claim supported or precise uncertainty. No blanket bootstrap auditor, repeated source re-opening or all-or-nothing role bounce for unaffected facts.

AGENTS.md owns shared authority, evidence reuse and task boundaries.

## Matt Pocock method extension — 2026-10-10

Define the actual question and what evidence would settle it. Prefer primary sources, compare conflicting claims and distinguish direct findings from inference. Inspect referenced code/documents when they are the best source, verify time-sensitive facts and attach citations to the claims they support. Finish with the answer, relevant evidence, uncertainty and decision implication rather than a search transcript. Parallel research is optional only when delegation is explicitly permitted; do not manufacture citations or turn research into implementation authority.
