# Project Initiation and Decomposition

## Trigger

New or resumed work needs a usable plan.

## Method

Keep the outcome and acceptance in its parent. Use an inline Work plan checkbox list for small work. Use one linked plan for substantial sequencing/dependencies, with Objective, Work plan and Next executable steps. Preserve existing history and exact parent/backlink integrity.

## Stop

Next executable action is clear. No mandatory separate plan, 10-section template or capacity/RAID forecast for a small Ticket.

AGENTS.md owns shared authority, evidence reuse and task boundaries.

## Intake-to-plan handoff — 1 November 2026

Use PLAY-0021's existing/new-target and Library-reuse assessment before decomposing newly scoped work. Reuse accepted outcomes, relevant knowledge, requirements and the existing current-phase entry; do not run a second interview or create a second requirements packet. Give the next useful slice its observable result and required evidence, and preserve unresolved dependencies instead of inventing missing inputs.

The parent's overall phase and each child Ticket's phase may differ. Track the phase of the actual item; do not mark the entire Project verified because one child passed. New evidence reopens only affected requirements, discovery or approach and their dependent steps. A small Ticket can keep this in its inline plan without separate phase documents or a mandatory prototype.
