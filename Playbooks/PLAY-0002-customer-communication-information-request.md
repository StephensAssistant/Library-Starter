# Customer Communication — Information Request

## Trigger

Work needs facts held by another person.

## Method

Resolve facts available in current records first. Under [[PLAY-0034]], ask once for the minimum remaining facts, distinguishing a new request from an already outstanding one.

## Stop

Facts arrive or a send-ready draft is delivered. the Library owner reviews and sends it; record a waiting owner or next action only after the Library owner confirms it was sent.
