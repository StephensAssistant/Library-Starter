# Workflows — FLOW

A reusable orchestration contract connecting Playbooks and nested Workflows into an end-to-end process. Skills supply individual capabilities; Playbooks combine Skills for one defined outcome; Hats own or review steps across every layer. Order, conditions and handoffs determine the process, not simply membership of a list.

the Library owner approved this governed type, automated structural checks, independent reviewer and foundation-first/pilot-first adoption on 16 October 2026. This is Library content, separate from GitHub Actions under .github/workflows.

## Canonical format and lifecycle

Use FLOW-<NNNN>-<slug>.md, copied from _TEMPLATE.md after reading _HOW-TO-CREATE.md. Reserve actual FLOW IDs through the existing serialised allocator; never guess an ID. One Markdown record contains the human explanation and exactly one fenced workflow-json contract. The contract is the single source for states and transitions; derive diagrams from it rather than maintaining a second flow.

status is current or superseded. review_status is draft, needs_review or approved. Current means the record is live guidance; it does not mean approved, tested or authorised for execution. Drafts may preserve explicit gaps, but cannot be invoked. A source save, a reviewer PASS and a score each grant no execution, send, spending, scheduling, deployment or merge authority.

Keep per-run state, current permissions, answers, evidence, attempts and handoffs in the owning Ticket/Project and its used plan. Record the FLOW ID, exact definition hash and dependency baseline. Do not store customer data, secrets or mutable run state in reusable workflow instructions.

## Required design contract

Every workflow states purpose, trigger, scope, non-goals, typed or otherwise unambiguous inputs/outputs, current authority limits, completion criteria, persistence owner and residual risks. Define acceptance before composing steps.

Each material step identifies its kind, accountable Hat, execution identity/access evidence, scope, prerequisites, inputs/outputs, permitted effects, observable success check, controls, bounded attempts and recovery. Every step and control state names its existing governing Playbook in the playbook field. An executable playbook step must use that same PLAY ID. A nested workflow call still has a governing Playbook for invocation, return and recovery, and names its reusable FLOW subprocess in uses. Decision, wait and terminal states are controls of their governing Playbook, not permission to invent free-form work. Declare additional required capabilities by existing IDs. Decisions have explicit branches, exactly one otherwise path, and a failure destination. Waits have a resume condition and a timeout. Nonterminal steps always have a failure destination. Every FLOW has exactly two service terminal states: completed and awaiting_steve. Blocked, failed, cancelled and stopped describe internal attempt conditions, not additional service endpoints. For failure-to-handoff ordering and cancellation, apply Playbooks/PLAY-0069-workflow-qualification-and-recovery.md, Two-outcome service contract.

Use finite max_transitions for the whole invocation, including loops, and max_attempts of 1–3 for a step. A retry needs changed input or new verified evidence; the bound is a ceiling, never permission to retry. Nested workflow steps require return_policy completed_only and budget_policy shared: only a child completed terminal follows the parent next edge; a child awaiting_steve return, failed/cancelled attempt, failed admission or timeout follows parent failure and preserves child evidence/effects. Apply PLAY-0069 Two-outcome service contract to the child packet and root handoff. Nested workflow calls cannot be recursive and share the parent's remaining transition budget; they do not reset authority, attempts or elapsed-time limits. The runner/operator must enforce and record these limits; the source checker only verifies their declaration.

No unresolved required permission, identity/access, evidence, contradictory instruction, unmet prerequisite or missing verification may be bypassed. Block only the dependent path; continue independent authorised discovery/work where safe. A failure branch must preserve evidence and effects already applied, define safe re-entry/idempotency, and identify the responsible owner. Never substitute a compensating write, rollback, broader scope or new authority without evidence and authorisation.

## Outcome, necessary work, decisions, recovery and proof — mandatory design framework

This section owns the five-part design framework. The template, creation guide and supporting Skills apply it; they must not define different completion or authority rules. Use MUST for a requirement, MUST NOT for a prohibition, and MAY ONLY for an option whose conditions are stated. Replace vague terms such as "appropriate", "sufficient", "as needed" and "if successful" with a named rule, measurable predicate or a referenced canonical criterion. These words are not predicates by themselves.

**No inferred contract.** Every required value must be supplied by an identified authoritative source or by the decision owner. Record the source/revision and any required freshness. A missing, contradictory or unevaluable required value is a gap; do not invent a default, silently select a branch or report a PASS. Investigate accessible evidence, then use the existing recovery/decision route for the dependent action. A deliberately permitted default must state its exact triggering condition and value. Draft placeholders are allowed only while review_status is draft or needs_review; they block approval and invocation. A workflow author must not invent business policy to complete the template.

### 1. Outcome — define acceptance before steps

Record the beneficiary, requested change in the actual environment, exact deliverable/target, in-scope population or time window, exclusions, accountable owner and current authority. Give each acceptance requirement a workflow-local key (not a new Library ID), an observable predicate, verification procedure, evidence source and freshness rule. Specify the values/tolerances and units that determine PASS or FAIL; if a judgement is necessary, name its rubric and decision owner. A requirement without an evaluable predicate blocks approval. Completion requires every mandatory predicate and governing gate to pass against the intended final state. An action's return code, completed repair, matching diagram or model statement alone cannot establish that state. Keep completed and awaiting_steve as the only service outcomes; do not change the shared cancellation policy.

Put the outcome in purpose, population/targets in scope and inputs, deliverables/proof in outputs, the all-required acceptance predicate in completion, and the owner/evidence location in persistence. Use these existing workflow-json fields; do not invent unsupported schema keys or duplicate transitions in prose.

### 2. Necessary work — justify retained operations

Map each step and its finite operations to at least one acceptance requirement, required dependency or named material-risk control. A required dependency must identify which later requirement/operation needs its output. A risk control must identify the risk, consequence and governing requirement; "best practice" alone is not a reason to add work. Remove an operation with no such mapping, unless a governing contract explicitly requires it. One step may satisfy several requirements; do not add a separate phase or ledger merely for every heading in this framework.

For each operation state input identity/freshness, responsible executor, permitted action/effects, required output/evidence, success predicate and existing next/failure/return transition. Reuse qualifying evidence under the four minimum-execution rules below. Added tests, reviews, source files, context reads and user interruptions obey the same necessity rule. Parallel work MAY ONLY be used when authorised, its dependencies and shared effects are declared, and its join/acceptance owner is named. A shorter diagram or more workers is not evidence of lower cost.

### 3. Decisions — make every branch evaluable

Apply SKILL-0320. For every decision define input types/domains and provenance, exact conditions including boundaries, the match-selection rule, destination and treatment of absent, stale, malformed or conflicting data. Define whether multiple matches are prohibited or intentionally combined/ordered. Require exactly one otherwise branch in the graph; otherwise is the explicit no-match rule, not permission to swallow an evaluation error. Missing required evidence and failed condition evaluation follow the declared failure/recovery path. Human judgement uses a named rubric/owner; it must not be represented as an objective calculation. Unknown facts remain unknown. Use SKILL-0318's current clarification cadence for genuine the Library owner-owned decisions; execution remains one domino at a time.

Apply SKILL-0321 to feasible state/guard combinations, parallel joins and waits. A visible path to a terminal is not proof that its conditions can be satisfied. Preserve the generated visual as an exact view of workflow-json; semantic and runtime evidence remain separate.

### 4. Recovery — preserve effects and remaining limits

For each material failure identify its observable detection, failed requirement, cause versus uncertainty, completed/uncertain effects, canonical defect owner and the existing PLAY-0069 route. Distinguish a retryable request failure from invalid input, denied authority/access, a stale deterministic result and an uncertain mutation. Define finite request/attempt/page and time bounds before the relevant operation; state which layer owns retries and carry shared counters without reset. Bind cancellation at this failure boundary to PLAY-0069 Two-outcome service contract.

Before retrying a mutation whose result is uncertain, reconcile the actual target or use an evidenced idempotency/deduplication contract; absence of a response is not proof of no effect. Recovery must identify the exact affected step, prerequisites for continuation, invalidated evidence and required rechecks. Reuse unaffected evidence only under the governing identity/scope/freshness rules. Record repair acceptance separately from original outcome acceptance. No recovery branch revives a terminated invocation, expands authority or converts a failure to completed success.

### 5. Proof — define the oracle before execution

Map every acceptance requirement and material failure control to its independently specified expected result, verification method, named execution owner, exact input/definition revision, observed result and one canonical evidence pointer. Classify evidence as structural/static, synthetic scenario, actual fixture, actual platform or independent review. State what each proves and what remains untested; none substitutes for another when the governing contract requires it. A designed test is not an executed test. An independent reviewer may assess existing execution evidence without duplicating it under the four minimum-execution rules.

Qualify normal completion, each materially different decision/failure boundary, missing/contradictory evidence, partial/uncertain effects, recovery exhaustion, child return, cancellation and prohibited scope as applicable to actual mechanisms. An omitted case needs a concrete mechanism-based non-applicability reason in existing evidence, not a blanket "not applicable". Exact visual parity and graph reachability do not establish semantic soundness, external success or exactly-once effects. Approval requires the existing independent qualification gates; this framework adds no automatic reviewer or runtime enforcement.

Use SKILL-0093 for outcome evaluation and SKILL-0132 for flow measures. Declare only the measures needed by the intended acceptance or efficiency question, with numerator/denominator or duration boundaries, units, population, source, window and owner. Candidate measures are accepted completion rate, first-pass completion, end-to-end time including waits, cost per accepted completion and avoidable rework/interventions. Separate measured results from proposed targets and design estimates; do not invent a baseline or claim an improvement without comparable observations. Hard authority/safety constraints cannot be traded away through an average score.

**Author/reviewer rejection rule:** withhold approval for any missing required outcome/predicate, unjustified operation, ambiguous branch/default, undefined recovery/effect handling, absent required proof or contradictory active instruction. Repair the smallest affected owner and callers, preserve historical evidence, and obtain exact required re-review. Existing approved workflows whose changed dependencies invalidate acceptance become needs_review; do not refresh pins or carry forward a PASS without qualification.

## Guardrail and gap review

Apply HAT-0022 and SKILL-0313. For every control record:
- requirement and consequence;
- mode: enforced, instruction or unverified;
- actual enforcement location/evidence, or explicit unverified state;
- whether the control is critical to proceeding.

A prose rule is not runtime enforcement. Procedural controls may be appropriate for manual steps, but critical runtime controls cannot be represented as enforced without representative runtime evidence. Missing required controls block the affected action regardless of score.

For every gap, search current owners and relevant supporting/conflicting evidence:
1. Existing capability covers the need: reuse it.
2. Same playbook outcome, incomplete procedure/control: amend its existing owner.
3. Distinct reusable outcome: propose/create a Playbook through its governed process.
4. Missing individual technique: reuse/amend its Skill; create a new Skill only when no owner fits.
5. Missing access, human decision or implementation evidence: keep that dependency blocked, with owner and next action.

Do not create every missing component automatically regardless of authority. Complete authorised bounded repairs, verify them, then re-review. Maintain one canonical rule, not copied mutable instructions in multiple layers.

## Minimum necessary execution contract — 17 October 2026

These four rules apply to every phase, called workflow and shared recovery operation. They are mandatory operator and author/reviewer requirements. They do not add an executor, relax a required check or grant authority.

1. **Repair the failed requirement only.** Before repair, identify the actual failed check, its expected/observed result and exact input, then name only the requirements/callers whose acceptance depends on that failure or the proposed correction. Investigate and change that affected set only. Reuse existing evidence for unaffected requirements after verifying its exact input/source identity, applicable scope, required freshness, and absence of contradictory results. A current canonical Git blob identity may establish unchanged bytes from an already verified snapshot; do not redownload unchanged files merely to rediscover them. A changed instruction/policy still invalidates its approval: renew exact affected qualification and independent acceptance; never relabel an old PASS as current. Rerun a check only when its contract requires a fresh execution, its tested input changed, its evidence is missing/invalid/expired, or a specific contradiction makes that result unreliable. Mandatory current admission, actor/access/ref checks, complete exact-candidate preparation/release checks and required independent review remain mandatory. Close recovery when the failed requirement and affected acceptance checks pass and the qualified handback is delivered; do not replay completed phases or investigate another possible cause without new failure evidence. All original stop, authority and cumulative repair limits remain.

2. **One execution owner for each test group.** Head assigns one named person/agent to execute each distinct set of assertions against an identified input revision. Record the command/procedure, input identity, expected/observed result, coverage and one evidence location. Reuse that result across callers. The independent reviewer must assess test adequacy, source identity, actual results and their limits; reviewer independence does not itself require repeating the tests. Before any duplicate execution, record one of these concrete reasons: an explicit independent-execution requirement; an untested changed input/assertion; missing, invalid or expired execution evidence; or an identified contradictory/unreliable result. Repeat only the affected assertions unless a governing check explicitly requires the full suite. ## Single-owner rule bindings

Each mutable instruction has one authoritative existing owner. In a FLOW, template, guide or supporting Skill, use a canonical path/ID and exact stable section, state when it applies, and supply only the caller's actual operation, input/evidence, permitted effects, acceptance or parameters. Do not restate the owner's predicates, limits or repair procedure. Distinct local parameters and mechanically required schema fields are bindings, not a reason to copy prose policy. Historical evidence and generated representations remain clearly labelled and cannot govern current execution.

Before dependent effects, resolve the mandatory reference against the current admitted dependency/policy/resource baseline and apply its current contract. Reuse already verified current reads; recover missing context. A missing, stale, ambiguous or contradictory required reference denies the dependent effect and follows PLAY-0069's existing recovery/handoff; it never defaults to copied wording. This is an operator requirement, not an installed reference resolver. Required dependency loading, hashes and exact independent acceptance retain their existing mechanisms.

Author/reviewer gate: reject a second mutable definition, unresolved owner/reference, circular decision prerequisite, or binding that omits a local obligation. Map any removal to its retained owner and verify affected normal/failure/boundary decisions; a shorter file alone is not acceptance. For a shared-rule migration, prove a predeclared discriminating scratch-only owner change is used by affected consumers without editing their policy prose, with stale/missing-reference refusal and separate admission evidence. Retain opening/per-step mini briefings through Mandatory operating briefings. Changing an owner still invalidates affected approval: mechanical pins, generated evidence and required reviews may need renewal although the rule itself is authored once.
